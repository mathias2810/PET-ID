
# 🐾 PET-ID
### Your Pet's Digital Identity

**PET-ID** is a pet identification and recovery system designed to help owners protect their pets and increase the chances of finding them if they get lost.

The project combines a physical identification tag with a QR code linked to a digital profile containing essential information about the pet and its owner.

## 🚨 The Problem

Every day, pets go missing, and many struggle to return home because they lack reliable identification or their owners cannot be contacted.

Traditional identification tags often contain limited information, such as a phone number, which can become outdated or may not provide enough information to ensure a safe reunion.

PET-ID aims to address these challenges by making pet identification more accessible, practical, and efficient.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

PET-ID offers a smart identification system that connects a physical QR code tag to a digital pet profile.

By scanning the QR code with a smartphone, anyone who finds a lost pet can access the available information and contact the owner without needing to install an application.

Our goal is to improve pet safety, simplify identification, and support the recovery of lost animals.

---

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Register the pet:** The owner creates a digital profile with the pet's information.
2. **Create the identification tag:** A unique QR code is linked to the pet's profile.
3. **Attach the tag:** The owner places the tag on the pet's collar.
4. **Scan the QR code:** Anyone who finds the pet can scan the code using a smartphone.
5. **View the profile:** The finder sees the information the owner has chosen to share.
6. **Contact the owner:** The finder uses the available contact options to report the pet's location.
7. **Recover the pet:** The owner and finder coordinate the reunion.

## 🐶 Digital Pet Profile

Each registered pet will have a digital profile that may include:

- Pet's name.
- Photograph.
- Species and breed.
- Age or date of birth.
- Color and distinguishing characteristics.
- Medical information relevant to emergencies.
- Special care instructions.
- Owner contact information.
- Identification and registration details.

The owner will be able to update the information when necessary.

Sensitive personal information should only be displayed with the owner's authorization.

---

## 🔎 Lost Pet Mode

When a pet goes missing, the owner can activate **Lost Pet Mode**.

This feature is intended to make it easier for someone who finds the animal to identify it and contact its owner.

Potential features include:

- A visible lost-pet alert on the digital profile.
- The date and general area where the pet was last seen.
- A form for reporting where the pet was found.
- Contact options for the owner.
- Instructions for safely approaching or handling the pet.

The system should avoid publishing the owner's exact home address or other sensitive information.

## 1. Introduction

TAM, SAM, and SOM are tools that help entrepreneurs measure the potential market for a business. They make it easier to identify possible customers, choose a target area, and establish realistic business objectives.

For PET-ID, these concepts can help estimate the number of pet owners who could benefit from a digital identification system designed to help recover lost pets.

## 2. What Is TAM?

TAM means **Total Addressable Market**. It represents the total potential market for a product or service if every possible customer could use it.

### Application to PET-ID

The TAM for PET-ID includes pet owners who want to protect their dogs and cats with an identification system. The product could include a QR-code tag connected to a digital profile containing the pet's name, photograph, owner's contact details, and relevant information.

If a pet gets lost, someone who finds it could scan the QR code and contact its owner more easily.

For example, suppose the business identifies 100,000 potential customers across Ecuador. If each customer purchases one identification tag for $8, the theoretical TAM would be $800,000 in one-time sales.

**Calculation:**

100,000 customers × $8 = **$800,000**

*This is an illustrative estimate, not a verified count of pet owners in Ecuador.*

## 3. What Is SAM?

SAM means **Serviceable Available Market**. It is the part of the total market that the business can reach with its current product, location, and resources.

### Application to PET-ID

At the beginning, PET-ID could focus on pet owners in Quito. This would make it easier to promote the product, deliver the identification tags, and establish relationships with veterinary clinics and pet shops.

Suppose the business estimates that 20,000 pet owners in Quito could be potential customers.

If each customer purchases one tag for $8, the SAM would be $160,000 in potential one-time sales.

**Calculation:**

20,000 customers × $8 = **$160,000**

The business could reach these customers through social media, veterinary clinics, pet adoption events, and partnerships with animal shelters.

## 4. What Is SOM?

SOM means **Serviceable Obtainable Market**. It is the part of the serviceable market that a business can realistically capture within a specific period.

### Application to PET-ID

During its first year, PET-ID could aim to sell 500 identification tags. This goal would be more realistic than trying to reach every pet owner in Quito immediately.

The project could begin with a simple website, affordable QR-code tags, and a digital profile for each registered pet. The team could also promote the product through demonstrations and partnerships with local veterinarians.

If 500 customers each purchase one tag for $8, the first-year sales target would be $4,000.

**Calculation:**

500 customers × $8 = **$4,000**

*This figure represents projected sales before production, marketing, delivery, and other business expenses. It is a planning target, not a guarantee of sales.*
